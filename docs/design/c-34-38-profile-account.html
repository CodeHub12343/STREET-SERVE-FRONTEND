Popular questions
How does the line-up discount work?⌄
The earlier you join a vendor's line, the bigger your discount — locked in the moment you join, and applied automatically at checkout. Each new customer gets a bigger cut until the vendor's cap.
What happens if I don't return consignment inventory?⌄
You're responsible for its declared value until it's returned or sold. Missing a return deadline affects your Trust Score and may reduce future limits — see your Seller Agreement.
Is my location shared with vendors?⌄
Only when you wave someone down or go live as a seller. Your precise location is never shown to other users — it's fuzzed on the map.
How do payouts and timing work?⌄
Payout speed depends on your verification tier: Bronze holds 3 days, Silver is next-day, Gold is instant.

C-34 — the profile is the launch pad for other roles

  • Per the client's 5-tab nav, Jobs/Sell and Wallet live here rather than as top-level tabs — so the profile leads with "Do more on StreetServe" (open seller mode / set up a business), making those paths prominent despite being one level down.
  • Role chips show the additive-roles model at a glance (Customer + Seller), and the stat strip surfaces the numbers that matter across roles — waves, Trust Score, earnings — reusing the boxed-stat pattern from the AI card fix.

C-35 — one wallet, three money relationships

  • Balance (from ping tips + round-up change), payment methods, Spot Me obligations you owe, and ping earnings — the wallet honestly shows money flowing in and out, including debts, rather than hiding what you owe.
  • Spot Me shows the agreed repay-by date in amber — a reputation-based nudge, never a debt-collection tone, consistent with the platform ethos.

C-36 — the tier ladder, made legible

  • Bronze/Silver/Gold as a vertical ladder with done/current/locked states — a user always sees exactly where they are and what unlocks next (the same explainability principle applied to Trust Score and AI).
  • The rejected-document state (blurry ID) is the error case from Flow 1: specific reason + re-upload, and status is preserved in the interim rather than dropped.
  • Restates that ID docs are provider-held, never stored by StreetServe (Security §2) — right where a user is about to hand one over.

C-37 — safety-critical notifications can't be silenced

  • Per-category notification toggles, but payout/dispute alerts show an "Email" chip instead of a switch — they can be redirected but never fully muted (Flow 12), so a user never misses money or a dispute.
  • Location precision and "show me as a live seller" are first-class privacy controls, and "Download my data" / "Delete account" satisfy CCPA-style rights given the California pilot.

C-38 — help routes into the real dispute flow

  • FAQs answer the questions this specific product generates (line-up discount, consignment liability, location privacy, payout timing) — not generic boilerplate.
  • "Open a dispute" is a real entry point into the dispute case object (the customer side of the Admin A-02 queue), not a dead-end contact form.

Handoff notes

  • C-34 role actions call `POST /auth/roles`; the stat strip reads per-role Trust Scores (separate per role, Q10).
  • C-35 payment methods proxy to Stripe (never raw card data, Security §3); Spot Me rows read `spot_me_requests`; ping earnings read `pings`.
  • C-36 re-upload re-initiates the KYC provider flow (Persona/Stripe Identity), updating `verification_records` on the provider webhook.
