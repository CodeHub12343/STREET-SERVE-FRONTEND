C-26 — three single-focus steps

  • WizardFlow discipline: service, then time, then review — never all three on one screen. Step dots in the header track progress the same way S-02's verification wizard does, one shared pattern across the whole design system.
  • Unavailable time slots are shown struck-through rather than hidden — a customer should see the full schedule shape (busy vs. open), not wonder why 1 PM is simply missing.
  • Recurring toggle is a plain switch, off by default — a one-time booking should never accidentally become a subscription.

Mobile-specific framing

  • "Where: We'll come to you" is stated plainly in the review step — the single biggest difference between booking a mobile service and booking a normal storefront appointment, so it can't be assumed or missed.
  • Confirmation screen explicitly says "add to calendar" + "reminders set" as two distinct confirmed actions (FR-7.2's 24h/1h reminder requirement), not a single vague "you're all set."

C-27 — the vendor doesn't just accept silently

  • Per FR-7.1, a vendor can accept, propose an alternate time, or decline — this detail screen models all three as distinct card states (simulate below), not just a binary confirmed/cancelled.
  • Proposed alternate time puts the decision back in the customer's hands with two clear options, never auto-accepts on the customer's behalf.
  • Vendor cancellation pairs the bad news with a concrete compensation offer in the same card (Flow 6's edge case: "potential compensation... e.g., discount on rebook") — never just an apology with no next step.

Reminder transparency

  • Reminder list shows sent vs. pending explicitly with real send times — makes the 24h/1h promise from the confirmation screen verifiable after the fact, not just a claim.

Handoff notes

  • Booking writes to `bookings` (Database §2): scheduled_at, status, recurrence_rule, reminder_sent_24h/1h flags — the detail screen's reminder list reads directly from those two boolean fields.
  • Vendor propose-alternate is a status transition the vendor dashboard (V-07 Bookings Calendar) initiates; this screen is the customer-facing mirror of that action.
