Snap behavior (§2.6f)

  • Peek — identity + status + Wave Down/Directions. Identical to what C-10 established; no new learning.
  • Half — adds cover, action row, the queue/discount card, and Today's Special. Content clipped, not scrollable — pull to full for the rest.
  • Full — edge-to-edge, scrollable; drag handle disappears, compact header (close + name + status dot) takes over. Sticky CTA bar persists at every snap.
  • Dismiss — scrim tap or drag down from half; full requires the header close.

Queue & discount card

  • The line-up discount engine surfaced pre-decision: consumed tiers strike through, the customer's would-be tier highlights in --status-discount violet.
  • Copy is possessive and time-bound ("you'd be 4th", "locks in 15%") — the FOMO is the feature.
  • Hidden entirely when Closed: never show a queue that can't be joined.

Action row semantics

  • Follow toggles in place (filled = following) — ongoing relationship, feeds Favorites (Flow 2b).
  • Notify Me stays one-shot — fires a confirmation toast, no persistent state on the button. The two are deliberately different shapes so users learn the difference.
  • Directions deep-links to native maps at the business's live location; Message opens a scoped thread (C-33).

Status-adaptive rules

  • One source of truth drives cover treatment, chip, location line, queue card visibility, and CTA swap (Wave Down ⇄ Notify Me) — switch to Closed to see all five change together.
  • Order disables when Closed; Today's Special dims but stays visible (tomorrow's reason to come back).
  • Location line phrasing per status: "Heading your way — near…" / "Parked — Main St & 5th Ave" / "Closed — opens tomorrow 10 AM".

Handoff notes

  • Cover 16:9, menu thumbs 4:3, gallery 1:1 (§2.6g); emoji are placeholder art.
  • Reviews show "verified purchases" — transaction-gated per the review-manipulation rule (Security §4).
