Two contexts, one screen set

  • At the Window (arriving from C-20's queue resolution): the line already was the wait, so payment leads straight to the receipt — no separate tracker. Discount line is present and framed as the payoff for having waited.
  • Order Ahead (Direct Order, Flow 2d): no queue discount by default, but gets the full C-23 pending→accepted→ready pipeline since the vendor hasn't seen this order yet and genuinely needs to act on it.
  • Toggling context above the phone changes both which rows appear in the cart and what payment leads to — reflecting that C-21/22 are shared, C-23 is conditional.

Fee transparency (FR-11.3)

  • The full platform/vendor/tip split lives in a collapsed disclosure ("Where this goes"), not a bare line item in the main total — satisfies FR-11.3's full-transparency requirement without making the customer's primary total look like it has a surprise fee bolted on.
  • The headline total customers see is simply what left their card; the split is one tap away for anyone who wants it, which is the "make trust visible, not just enforced" UX principle applied literally.

Discount payoff moment

  • "You saved $3.15 by being customer #4" repeats verbatim from the queue screens (C-20) through cart, receipt banner, and the itemized row — one consistent number the customer can track the whole way, never recalculated or restated differently.

Failure states

  • Card decline: "nothing was taken — your order is held, not lost" — mirrors the wave-down journey's reassurance pattern; retry or cancel, never silently retried.
  • Order cancelled mid-tracking (business went Away/Closed, PRD edge case): cancelled card replaces the stepper, explicit no-charge statement, refund implied since payment was already held not captured until ready — simulate it below the phone on the Tracking screen.
  • Processing state uses an actual spinner — the one place in the system a spinner (not a skeleton) is correct, per §2.6e, since it's a blocking financial submission.

Handoff notes

  • Idempotency key sent with the Pay action (API §16) — a retried tap after a network blip must never double-charge; this is invisible in the UI by design.
  • Tracking steps map directly to `orders.status` (pending/accepted/ready/completed/cancelled, Database §3); the active-step glow is the only non-essential motion here and collapses under reduced motion.
  • Star rating on the receipt is the entry point to C-16's full review composer, gated to completed transactions only (anti-manipulation rule, Security §4).
